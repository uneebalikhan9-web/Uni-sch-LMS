# Lancers Tech LMS - Portals & Modules Requirements

## 1-VC (Vice Chancellor / Super Admin)
**📊 University-wide Analytics & KPIs**
* Enrollment & Retention Metrics (active students, YoY growth, dropout rates, funnel, intake comparison)
* Academic Performance Overview (GPA distribution, pass/fail rates, grade inflation, top/bottom programs)
* Faculty Comparison Analytics (benchmarking, staff-to-student ratio, teaching vs research, budget efficiency)
* Research Output Tracking (publications, citations, active projects, grants, patents)

**🎓 Student Lifecycle Management**
* At-Risk Student Monitoring (AI-flagged, warning scores, interventions, counseling)
* Satisfaction & Wellbeing Scores (surveys, mental health, complaints, safety, engagement index)
* Graduation & Employability Rates (on-time rate, time-to-degree, employment rate, sector breakdown)
* Financial Aid Overview (scholarships vs budget, need vs merit, unpaid fees, donor tracking)

**👩‍🏫 Faculty & Staff Management**
* Faculty Performance Metrics (evaluations, delivery quality, research, absenteeism, promotions)
* Staff Workload Distribution (teaching hours, admin vs academic, overload alerts, adjunct ratio)
* HR & Recruitment Pipeline (open positions, candidate pipeline, time-to-hire, onboarding)
* Professional Development Tracking (CPD hours, training enrollment, compliance, skill gaps)

**💰 Financial Governance**
* Budget vs Actual Spend (tracking per cost center, variance reports, overspend alerts, projections)
* Revenue Streams Breakdown (tuition collection, grants, research funding, donations, partnerships)
* Grant & Funding Management (active grants, deadlines, success rates, expired/renewed)
* Financial Forecasting (end-of-year models, cash flow, scenario planning, capex)

**🏛 Academic Quality & Compliance**
* Accreditation Status Tracker (HEC, ISO, audit readiness, renewals, non-conformance)
* Curriculum Review Status (programs under review, last update date, approval workflows)
* Exam & Assessment Integrity (plagiarism rates, grade appeals, invigilation incidents)
* Policy Compliance Monitoring (acknowledgments, data protection, health & safety)

**🌐 Institutional Strategy & Rankings**
* National & Global Ranking Tracker (QS, THE, HEC, historical trends, gap analysis)
* Strategic Plan KPI Progress (RAG status, milestones, accountability mapping)
* International Partnerships (MoUs, exchanges, joint degrees, partnership health)
* Alumni Engagement Metrics (registered alumni, events, donations, mentorship)

**⚙ Operations & Infrastructure**
* Campus Facility Utilization (occupancy rates, conflicts, maintenance, asset lifecycle, energy)
* LMS Platform Health Metrics (uptime, daily active users, support tickets, feature adoption)
* IT & Cybersecurity Status (server status, incidents, data breaches, software licenses)
* Sustainability & ESG Metrics (carbon footprint, energy/water, recycling, SDGs)

**📋 Governance & Executive Tools**
* Board & Senate Report Center (auto-generated reports, agendas, minutes, committees)
* Risk Register (risk log, owners, mitigation plans, emerging flags)
* Announcements & Broadcast (composer, targeted messaging, delivery confirmation, alerts)
* Delegation & Approval Workflows (pending VC sign-offs, authority matrix, escalation)
